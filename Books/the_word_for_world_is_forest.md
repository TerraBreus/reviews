## The word for world is forest.
_Written by Ursula Le Guin_

Thought I already wrote this review but I guess not. Really loved this book. Le Guin writes with such empathy and understanding it is hard not to understand how the characters act and think.

What I think is so amazing about Le Guin her writing is that she doesn't divide between good and evil. Both the "good" and the "bad" people have their sides. 
I especially liked how at the end Selver admits that there is no going back from their knowing of the concept of murder (something they didn't knew as a concept and thereby were also a non-violent race).

(not a glowing review but who cares)

7/10
