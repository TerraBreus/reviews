# The Long Way Down
_by Nick Hornby_

With only two-hundred-and-fifty pages I thought this to be a short read.
At first the pacing seemed perfect; Short chapters, thoughts and views from one of the four protagonists , 
a low reading barrier... Like little cigarette breaks. 

I found myself chipping away at the book five minutes at a time.

However I struggled with finishing this book. Compared to more traditional books, the constant switching from perspective broke the flow for
long reading sessions. Instead of binging my way through the book, it was slowly digested.
This could have played out quite well, but I believe it lacked the depth to turn the black humor and playful writing into serious thinking material.

Overall I liked the book. Hornby subtly sprinkles the book with (boyish) recommendations on (rock) music, books and general views on how to enjoy life, but it 
failed to grab my attention and smack it onto the wall. Having four characters all have their own "coming-of-age" arch is quite a lot to take in and constantly relate to. 
Combine that with a witty writing style and you find yourself giggling while struggling to whole-heartedly engage emotionally with the characters.

6/10
