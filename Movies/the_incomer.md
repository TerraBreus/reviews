## The Incomer (2026)
_Directed and written by Louis Paxton_

So so charming with their silly casting, absurd humor and strange stories! Hats off to the actors (and to the casting director); everyone played their role so well. 

The whole story takes place pretty much on the one island which is also incredibly considering your average block-buster hollywood movie needs to travel half the world to get their "amazing" shots. 
Well, here is proof you don't need a shit ton of money to make a good movie. You just need talent and passion :) (_and scottish humor_)

Not too sure whether I would want to see this again by myself, but I wouldn't mind watching it a second time so I could introduce it to somebody.

**8/10**
