# Good Luck, Have Fun, Don't Die (2025)
_Directed by Gore Verbinski_

A huge and absurd clusterfuck of strange societal commentary that fails to take itself serious. 
When approached with a lighthearted attitude, this movie is highly entertaining with lovely dark humor and amazingly absurd visuals.

I thought it strange how absurd their reality was without the characters showing much skepticism on the absurdity of their situation. 
Some of the absurdity is part of the movie, yet they draw the line very, _very_** far to be able to accept. 
This lead to the thought that the entire movie might be a simulation/generated which felt like a big flaw in the movie, but perhaps I missed something.

Nonetheless entertaining, creative and funny. Just don't take it as food for thought.

7/10
