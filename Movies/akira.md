## Akira (1988)

Hmmmm... a couple of years ago I was shown this movie by a friend with the promise of it being one of the best animes out there. 
As per usual in my early twenties, we got high of our tits and, from what I remember, had an awesome time.

_Akira_ was reverberated in my mind as one of the better animes out there. So when it re-issue came along in the cinema's I thought it would be worth seeing again.

Turns out I was really _really_ high... While I will not disagree that Akira is unique and wonderfully drawn, I would certainly not hold it as one of the best animes.

The plotline is confusing, unfortunately quite dreading and there are **way** too many explosions.

6/10
