## Batman (1989)
_Directed by Tim Burton_

Christopher Nolan has probably made on my favorite movies when creating the series of Batman. However it was really cool to see the work done before him by Tim Burton.
After having seen this movie I now see I cannot give Nolan full credit as you can clearly see the influence made by Burton's making the whole Batman scene much more grim and dark.

Burton is the clear link between the child friendly comic that is Batman and the grim, awesome gruel version of Christopher Nolan. At times this movie feels a little
goofy or comical which makes sense if you consider the original comic. It feels a little like watching a grim child's play which weirdly enough makes it strange in a way that makes it rather difficult to take the movie completely serious. Again, comics...

If you can get over the goofy awkwardness, there is so much to enjoy.

NB: Scene setting (background scenery) was absolutely amazing.

7.5/10
