## The Invite (2026)
_Directed by Olivia Wilde_

I wouldn't call this movie fun, but I would say it is quite good. There's a lot of deliberately placed cringing which feels horrible but admittedly points the finger back at us.

Seth Rogen is probably the weakest link in this movie due to his complete lack of emotional ability. But then again, he plays the role of an emotionally disconnected husband...

**7/10**

_Don't think I would watch this again._
