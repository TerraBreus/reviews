# Omaha
_Directed by Cole Webley_

Reading about this movie I expected to be filled with tears. At times a tear was dropped however I had a lot of 
difficulty emphasizing with the father and therefore found the emotional scenes rather infuriating than sad.

Arguably, the movie takes a lot from _Aftersun_ with parental dynamics that show the inner child of the parent,
a lot of atmospheric shots and the existential "not-knowing what we're doing". Only _Omaha_ is not as strong and not as long
to show the true complexity and dynamic of the characters. It scratches the right spot but just not hard enough.

The best part of the movie was definitely when it ended, but not because it was finished...

7/10
