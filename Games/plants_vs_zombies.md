## Plants vs Zombies (ds)

So much fun!

There is little to criticize about the game in general. The progression is immediately rewarding, the music is lovely,
the animations are cute and the jokes crack like eggs making omelet. The amount of attention this game was given is immaculate, which 
made me continuously want to search for hidden gems and read every bit of information on the zombies/plants.

Plants vs Zombies is a game anyone can play. Its audience is so broad that it seems to supersede the stereotype of a
videogame. It's a game not made for gamers perse. 

The frustration I sometimes felt from this game wasn't because the game was bad but because it was so good. 
You could sense that the developers wanted you to get annoyed by the bungee stealing b***** a** zombies taking exactly
the one plant you just saved up all your sun for... Fortunately the game is not as punishing that you have to restart
levels. You get angered but not upset. The difficulty is just right that you can finish most (if not all) levels 
without losing once. And still you will feel tense at times.

The only thing I would worth noting on the side of the DS is that having two screens does not add any gameplay 
whatsoever. This game is just as good as on your phone or any other device with a touch screen. I'm not sure
what they could have used the second screen for but only keeping the progression bar is a little too simple.
Perhaps it would have been nicer to keep the almanac or some other information on the second screen 
as a possible resource while fighting zombies. Nevertheless the game was as fun as can be.

---

In total I spend around 8 hours finishing the play through for the first time. 

After you finish the game for the first time Dave picks some seeds for you which makes your second play differ from the
first time. However it was the progression and discovering new plants/zombies that made me want to continue the game so
I don't think I'll be playing it for the second time.

The same goes with the mini-games. As there are no new plants or progressions to be made I feel little urge to play the game again.

8/10
