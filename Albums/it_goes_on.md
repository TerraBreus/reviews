## It goes on (2026)
_By Westside Cowboy_

At first this album glued itself to my ears. A comforting hug embracing life's struggles as a way of rebelling against the absurdity of it. 
The album was so catchy and strong that I continued to play it on repeat. Unfortunately, though the first layer of discovery was incredible, there were little layers to be found after.
A couple of days of endless listening and I got quite bored of it. In less than two weeks the album's needle no longer seemed to scratch the record of my mind and I found myself looking elsewhere.

I will recommend to come back every once in a while. It is a good record, in the same ball park as Canshaker Pi's _"Okay Decay"_ but a bit less nuanced, quieter and compressed. 
If Canshaker Pi is filter coffee, Westside Cowboy is green tea.

**Songs I didn't like as much:** _Big Wheels, Patchwork, You could have died there on the dancefloor_

**Songs I liked a lot:** _Kick Stones, Paperchains, Dobro, Worried Age (!), Coyote_

**6.5/10**
