## Second Gen Dream (2025)
_by forever_

Mixing elements of shoegaze and jungle, _Second Gen Dream_ feels like that breath of cold dry air on a setting winter evening. Cold but energizing. 
It makes me want to take my gloves off and accept the sharp wind cutting into my fingers.

The whole album only takes twenty two minutes and the songs transition quite nicely making the set a comfortable session without feeling the need to skip. 
This EP has definitely kept me going on the bike at times.

However, just like with most shoegaze, it does not stick for long. 
Even with the awesome jungle drumbeats it scratched an itch only for a short while. 
Still I like coming back to it every once in a while.

**7/10**

Written on 09/11
